How to read this evidence

Scores: Outcome correctness, legal reasoning correctness and legal basis correctness are separate answer-versus-Gold grades from 0 to 1; higher is better. Cards show primary replicate 1. Reliability repeats are displayed separately, never averaged.

Ranks: Each question has 15 separate four-answer comparisons, one per method/target. Rank 1 is best. Ties share occupied positions: two answers tied for first receive rank 1.5, not ranks 1 and 2. The numerical scores do not determine these independently judged ranks. The three controls can receive different ranks in different comparisons.

Limits: Paragraph IDs were supplied from ground truth, not predicted by a retrieval system. These machine judgments assess agreement with Gold; they do not verify retained-context grounding or replace lawyer assessment. The 18-answer view is not a global ranking of 18 candidates. No statistical tests or significance markers are added here.
